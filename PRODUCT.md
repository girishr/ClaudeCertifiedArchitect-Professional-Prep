# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing codebase: plain static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS served from GitHub Pages. `index.html` is generated by `tools/build_hub.py` from `tools/hub-template.html`, `questions.json` and a `PLAN` literal in the script. Course pages under `course/` are hand-edited HTML sharing `course/assets/lesson.css`, `nav.css` and `nav.js`. No framework, no bundler.

## Users

Candidates preparing for Anthropic's Claude Certified Architect - Professional (CCAR-P) exam. They work at Claude Partner Network organisations (a work email on a recognised domain is required to register). They arrive with a fixed amount of time, a paid exam slot ahead of them, and uneven confidence across seven weighted domains. They use the site in two distinct situations: long reading sessions on a laptop while working through a module, and short drill sessions to test recall.

## Product Purpose

A free companion to Anthropic's official five-module prep path (733 minutes). It distils the official material so it can be read rather than sat through, and adds the practice layer the official path does not provide: a day-by-day plan, a scenario question bank with per-distractor explanations, and per-domain scoring against the published exam weighting. Success is a candidate who knows which domains they are weak in and passes on the first attempt.

## Positioning

Built by someone who sat the exam, following the official path section by section in the official order, not a generic cert cram site. Every module title, description, duration and domain weighting is Anthropic's own, taken from the official exam guide; the site states plainly that the official course is authoritative and that nothing here replaces it. The practice engine scores against the published domain weighting rather than a flat percentage.

## Operating Context

- Public GitHub Pages site (`girishr.github.io/ClaudeCertifiedArchitect-Professional-Prep/`), public repo, CC BY 4.0.
- Two surfaces: the hub (`index.html`) as the workbench, with tabs for Course, Study plan, Practice, Results and Exam brief; the course (`course/`) as long-form reading with a persistent side nav.
- Progress and quiz state are held for the session only, not persisted across visits.
- Deep links into the hub use hashes (`#tab-plan`, `#tab-practice`); the shared side nav depends on them.
- Repo also ships the material as markdown (`notes/`, `labs.md`, `study-plan.md`) for reading outside the browser.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Practice engine: three modes (domain drill with instant feedback, 20-question timed quick set, 63-question 120-minute mock), 75 scenario questions weighted to the published domain split, per-question explanations and per-distractor notes.
- Results: estimated scaled score, overall accuracy, per-domain accuracy bars against the 72% pass mark, and a table view.
- Study plan: 21 days across three weeks, tickable, with a progress meter.
- Exam brief: confirmed exam facts and domain weightings from the official guide (obtained 20 Aug 2026).
- Light and dark themes, toggled and remembered in `localStorage`.
- Course: Module 1 fully distilled across twelve sections; Modules 2-5 are listed with official links only.
- Question bank contents, quiz mode behaviour, scoring maths and per-domain results logic are fixed and must not change.
- Prose copy, headings, explanations and exam facts are fixed and must not be rewritten.
- The fixed exam date (5 Sep 2026) and the calendar-dated study plan are the one exception: they are being reframed as a relative 21-day plan the visitor can start on any day, because the audience is now other candidates rather than one person with one booked slot.

## Brand Commitments

- The official Claude Certified Architect - Professional badge image (`assets/badge.png`) stays visible.
- Links to the official Anthropic Partner Academy path and exam guide stay present, and the statement that the official course is free, authoritative, and not replaced by this site stays visible.
- Product name in full: "Claude Certified Architect - Professional". Exam code CCAR-P.

## Product Principles

1. The official path is the spine; this site is the practice and revision layer around it, and says so.
2. Honest signal over encouragement: a mock holds feedback back until the end because that is the only way to get a real score.
3. Weighted by the real exam, not by convenience: every score and every study-time recommendation follows the published domain split.
4. Readable in one pass. Material is distilled to be read, not sat through, and revision sheets print well.
5. Nothing is claimed that the official guide does not confirm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- OpenDyslexic is already self-hosted and offered on course pages; that option must survive.
- Long-form reading and timed testing both need real contrast, keyboard-operable controls and honest focus states.
- Pages must print usefully; the pattern-selection sheet is explicitly a print target.
