Exam technique

  • Read the last sentence first, then the scenario. The ask is usually narrower than the setup suggests.
  • Find the binding constraint: a latency SLA, a compliance regime, a budget, a team maturity level, a residency rule. The right answer respects it.
  • On Select TWO, the two answers usually sit at different layers. Two from the same layer is a warning sign.
  • Distractors are real techniques applied at the wrong time. If two answers both look right, work out which constraint separates them.
  • Flag anything over 3 minutes and move on. A 10-minute reserve beats a perfect answer on question 12.
  • On governance and stakeholder questions, when in doubt pick the option that preserves the ability to undo.
