It is built from this kit's own domain notes, arranged to follow the official module's place in the path, and it has not been checked against Anthropic's screens. The official module is free, authoritative, and linked at the foot of this page; read it for the version that counts.

How to work through this

Read the eight in order once. They follow an engagement: discovery before the promise, the promise before the contract, the contract before the handover, the handover before the ramp, and the audience and the failure conversation last because they are what you do when the plan meets people. Sections 3 and 7 are the ones to come back to. Every page has a previous and a next.

WeightingWhere the value concentrates

Domain 6 is 14 percent, roughly nine questions of 63, and the official module is the second longest in the whole path at 178 minutes, longer than the production module that precedes it. Read that length as a deliberate weighting rather than padding. Technically strong candidates are the ones who arrive here underprepared, because the subject matter looks like common sense right up to the point where it is examined.

Four tables in this module are worth holding in memory: the SLA commitments in section 4, the build-versus-buy factors in the same section, the rollout phases in section 6, and the audience matrix in section 7. Items are written straight out of the last of those. The rest of the domain is sequencing practice: nearly every wrong answer is a reasonable consulting action taken at the wrong stage, or the most accommodating thing you could say to the person asking.
