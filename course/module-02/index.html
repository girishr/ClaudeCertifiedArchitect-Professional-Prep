It is built from this kit's own domain notes, arranged to follow the official module's place in the path, and it has not been checked against Anthropic's screens. The official module is free, authoritative, and linked at the foot of this page; read it for the version that counts.

How to work through this

Sections 2 to 7 are Domain 3, sections 8 to 11 are Domain 4, and they are ordered so each one narrows the last: mechanism, then the protocol, then the tools, then who holds the token, then retrieval, then what you can see when it goes wrong. Read them in order the first time. Together the two domains are roughly 22 of 63 questions, five or six of them multiple-response, so this is the module to give two evenings rather than one.

WeightingWhere the time actually goes

Domain 3 is 19 percent and Domain 4 is 16 percent, so the integration half is slightly heavier, but Domain 4 is where the multiple-response items cluster: "select TWO metrics", "select TWO grading methods". Those are the questions where a half-remembered list costs you the whole item, so sections 8 and 9 repay memorisation in a way the rest of the module does not.

Three sections carry more exam weight than their length suggests. Section 4 on tool design and capability bloat is the densest page here, and it is the one Anthropic's own engineering writing keeps returning to. Section 5 on authentication contains the MUST and MUST NOT rules that get quoted almost verbatim in questions. Section 11 on diagnosis is the page that turns an incident-report scenario into a single named failure mode, which is the exact shape most Domain 4 questions take.
