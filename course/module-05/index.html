How to work through this

One sitting. The whole module reads in about fifteen minutes and the official one is 45 minutes, the shortest in the path, because the domain is 7 percent of the paper. What it rewards is not understanding but recall: four ordered precedence lists, and one distinction between mechanisms that enforce and mechanisms that suggest. Read sections 1 to 6 in order, take the drill in section 3, then use section 7 as the page you come back to. Do not give this module a second evening. Give it to Module 2.

WeightingWhat 7 percent buys you

Seven percent of 63 questions is about four items. They are cheap items: two of them will most likely be a precedence order or a scope question, and precedence orders are memorisable in ten minutes. That is the best return per minute anywhere on the paper, which is exactly why it is worth doing properly once and then leaving alone.

The one place to slow down is section 3. Managed settings outrank command-line arguments, which is the inverse of how almost every other tool on your machine behaves, and an item written around that inversion catches people who reasoned from habit rather than from the list.
