Before you read

A partner says: we want an AI agent that handles our insurance claims. What is the first decision the architect makes?

Locked in. The section will not tell you whether you were right until the end.

Anthropic's Module 1 opens by naming four decisions. They happen before a line of code exists, they are made in order, and every later choice inherits them.

The module is not asking you to memorise these as stages. It is training you to recognise which of the four is in front of you, because each one rewards a different move. The instinct that serves you in decomposition is the wrong instinct when you are picking an entry point.

1.1What the module says you will be able to do

Six objectives, in the module's own framing, compressed. Before you read which decision each one trains, call it: which of the four decisions does the objective serve?

The role being trained

The architect who turns an ambiguous partner request into something someone can build, fund and defend. You are not writing the production code here. You are making the decisions that sit above it, in front of an enterprise buyer, often in a regulated setting, where a choice that looked clean in a demo turns up as a misroute in an audit three months later.
