How to work through this

Read the twelve sections in order the first time. They build: the model's properties come before decomposition, decomposition before the pattern, the pattern before the architecture, the architecture before the entry point. Every page has a previous and a next, so you can start at section 1 and never touch this page again.

WeightingWhere the time actually goes

Two screens carry a fifth of the official module between them: S21 on model and context strategy, and S28 on entry points and governance. S28 is the longest screen in the module by a wide margin, and section 10 looks small in any contents list. It is not. Budget by screen weight, not by section count.

The five Watch Out screens are the highest-value pages in the original. Each is a real architecture that failed, traced back to the decision that caused it, which is exactly the shape of an exam distractor. They are folded into sections 4, 5, 6, 8 and 10 here. Cover the diagnosis, decide what you would have done, then read.
