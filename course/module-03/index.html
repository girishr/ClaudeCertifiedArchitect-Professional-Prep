How to work through this

Read the eight in order once. They build: the stack before the gate, the gate before the failures it is there to stop, the failures before the obligations that make some of them illegal rather than merely bad, and the risk frame last because it is what you present. Section 5 is the one to come back to. Every page has a previous and a next.

WeightingWhere the value concentrates

Two sections are table-shaped and both tables are worth holding in memory. Section 2's guardrail stack is the frame every "which control" item is drawn against. Section 5's obligation-to-control mapping is the one items are written straight out of: a regulation is named, a scenario is given, and you choose the control set that satisfies it. Drill section 5 by reproducing its table on blank paper.

The rest of the domain is proportionality practice. Nearly every wrong answer here is a real control applied at the wrong severity, so the skill is not naming controls but pricing them against the harm in front of you.
