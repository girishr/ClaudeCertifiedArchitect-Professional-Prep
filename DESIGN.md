# Design System: CCAR-P Prep Kit

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Security Print, in Anthropic's Ink"**

This is a certificate press running on Anthropic's own colour and lettering. The craft is security printing — hairline rules, ruled ledgers, plate serials, engraved figures, a counterfoil, a guilloche rosette — because a weighted score report and a credential are both documents that earn trust by being unreproducibly precise. The stock and the inks, however, are read straight off anthropic.com: the warm slate-to-ivory ramp, clay as the single accent, olive and their error red as the two semantic inks, their focus blue on the focus ring. Dark is the steel plate and remains the default; light is ivory.

Density is high and deliberately unpadded. Nothing is a card floating in space; everything sits in a ruled structure that says where it belongs. Two surfaces share the world through `course/assets/print.css`, which `nav.js` injects on every page ahead of `nav.css` and any page stylesheet: the hub is a workbench set in the grotesque at a 15px px ramp, and the course is a reading sheet set in Faustina at a 17px root with a rem ramp. The originally specified didone display face is gone — display is now the grotesque at 700 with easier tracking — and the world lost nothing, because the engraving was never in the serifs; it is in the line work.

The build refuses the category arrangement it replaced: equal rounded cards on near-black with one blue accent and a progress bar. There are no shadows, no gradients, no radii, and no progress bar — the learning path's track is explicitly hidden and the count is printed instead.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm slate/ivory stock with exactly one accent ink, clay; olive reserved to the 720 pass mark.
- Square corners everywhere, including `rx: 0; ry: 0` on every SVG rect.
- Two line weights only: 1px hairline (`rule`) and 2px plate line (`rule-firm`).
- Flat by construction — depth is line density, never shadow or blur.
- Mono serials and tabular figures wherever the page labels or counts.
- Ornament lives in structure and never enters a question body or a timed screen.

## Colors

Warm, printed neutrals — nothing in the ramp is a cool grey — carrying one clay accent and three tightly-scoped semantic inks.

### Primary
- **Clay** (`tint` / `ink-fill`): Anthropic's accent, and the only one here. It is links, hover state, active tab underline, meter and bar fills, checked control dots, and the engraved line work of the rosette. In the dark theme the same value serves both line work and filled fields. In the light theme they split: line work and text take the deeper cut (`tint-light`), while `ink-fill` stays true clay as a filled field with slate text (`ink-fill-t`) on it.
- **Ink Fill Text** (`ink-fill-t`): slate, and the only thing that is ever set on a clay field.

### Secondary
- **Lifted Error Red** (`oxblood`): Anthropic's error red, lifted for the dark ground. Wrong answers, the low timer, flag labels, the caret, link hover. It marks a fault; it is never decoration.
- **Olive** (`mark`): the 720 pass mark and nothing else — the dashed ring in the rosette and the target line on each domain bar. Olive is the only ink with this job, and it is the only semantic green-yellow in the system.

### Tertiary
- **Focus Blue** (`focus`): Anthropic's focus blue, used by exactly one rule — the 2px `:focus-visible` outline at 2px offset in `print.css`. It is not available for any other purpose.
- **Unearned Ground** (`gap`): a mid neutral. In the rosette it engraves the distance between a domain's actual reach and the pass ring. A shortfall is unengraved ground, not an alarm.

### Neutral
- **Slate Plate** (`plate`): the ground of every dark surface; ivory (`plate-light`) in the light theme.
- **Raised Plate Face** (`sheet`) and **Well** (`sheet-2`): the two tonal steps above the ground, used for cards, wells, explanation blocks and stripes. They are the only way a surface reads as raised.
- **Ivory** (`ink`), **Soft Ink** (`ink-soft`), **Faint Ink** (`ink-faint`): the three text weights — body, secondary prose, and serial/label ink respectively.
- **Hairline** (`rule`) and **Plate Line** (`rule-firm`): the two rule weights, and also the border colours of every meter track, pill, counterfoil and input.
- **Quiet** (`quiet`) and **Dim Accent** (`accent-dim`): the two near-invisible washes — table stripes, and the active side-index row.
- **Code Ground** (`code-bg`): the only background code sits on.

Legacy aliases are deliberate and load-bearing; markup and older stylesheets name them. In `print.css`: `paper` (= `plate`), `accent` (= `tint`), `accent-dim`, `good` (= `mark`), `bad` (= `oxblood`), `flag` (= `sheet-2`), `code-bg`. In the hub: `grid` (= `rule`), `muted` (= `ink-faint`), `text-secondary` (= `ink-soft`), `success-text` (= `tint`), `critical` (= `oxblood`). `nav.css` resolves every colour through a three-deep fallback chain (course token, hub token, literal) so one menu works on both stylesheets.

### Named Rules
**The One Accent Rule.** Clay is the only accent in the system. A new element does not get a new hue; it gets clay, or an ink weight, or a rule.

**The Deep-Cut Clay Rule.** True clay `#d97757` measures 2.96:1 on ivory. In the light theme, therefore, text and line work take the deeper cut `#ad5533` and the true clay is used only as a filled field with slate text on it. Raising light-theme line work back to `#d97757` is not a correction; it is a contrast failure.

**The Reserved Mark Rule.** Olive means *720*. If a new element wants olive, it is claiming to be the pass mark; if it is not one, it takes `ink-faint`.

**The Ink-Fill Rule.** At most one filled colour field per sheet, and it is `ink-fill` with `ink-fill-t` on it — the hub's Module 1 block, or a lesson's `.path-start`. Hovering it empties the field and leaves the outline.

**The Bare-Plate Rule.** An empty state is unengraved plate: the low-density line work with nothing cut into it. Never a grey placeholder fill, never a zeroed bar dressed as data.

**The Unearned Ground Rule.** A shortfall is drawn in the neutral `gap`, not in red. Red is reserved for a wrong answer and an expiring clock.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Hanken Grotesk (variable, self-hosted) at 700 — `--cut`
**Body Font:** Faustina (variable, self-hosted, roman and italic) — `--read`
**UI Font:** Hanken Grotesk (variable, self-hosted) at 400–650 — `--ui`
**Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono (variable, self-hosted) — `--num`
**Reading option:** OpenDyslexic 400/700 (self-hosted; offered by `nav.js`, swaps the whole page and resets the root to 15.5px)

**Character:** A plain, wide-aperture grotesque does the announcing and the operating; a sturdy printing serif does the reading; a mono does every serial, figure and code run. The pairing is unhyped and legible at small sizes, which is what a workbench and a timed screen both need.

**Substitution, stated plainly.** Anthropic's own faces are Styrene A (`anthropicSans`) and Copernicus (`anthropicSerif`). Both are licensed and are **not** in this repo. Hanken Grotesk stands in for the sans and Faustina for the serif. JetBrains Mono is not a stand-in — it is the mono Anthropic actually ships. All four are self-hosted woff2 variable fonts under `course/assets/fonts/`, pulled from npm Fontsource, with `OFL-LICENSE-fontsource.txt` alongside; OpenDyslexic carries its own licence file. The three display/UI/mono faces this build previously used were deleted from the repo; the four faces above plus OpenDyslexic are the complete shipped set.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, `clamp(26px, 4.4vw, 44px)`, 1.02, `-0.012em`): the credential name in the hub masthead. One per page.
- **Display, sheet** (700, 2.5rem, 1.08): a lesson's `h1`.
- **Headline** (700, 1.35rem / 21px hub, `-0.008em`): section heads; `h2` on both surfaces. The section number sits inline in mono at `0.82em`, never stacked above the title.
- **Title** (700, 20px / 1.2rem): a ledger row's own heading.
- **Figures** (600, 40px hero / 21px counterfoil / 19px countdown, tabular): every printed number of consequence — the scaled score, the four plate facts, the days to go.
- **Standfirst** (Faustina, 1.16rem): the one paragraph under a display head.
- **Body, reading** (Faustina 400, 17px root, 1.62): lesson prose. 16px root below 34rem, 11pt in print.
- **Body, workbench** (grotesque 400, 15px, 1.55): hub prose and controls, stepping down 14 / 13.5 / 13 / 12.5px for subheads, rows, controls and notes.
- **Body, sheet apparatus** (grotesque, 0.9 → 0.78rem): tables, quiz, breadcrumbs, index, pager, captions on the reading sheet.
- **Label** (grotesque 600, 0.72 / 0.7 / 0.68 / 0.66rem, `0.12em`, uppercase, `ink-faint`): field and group labels.
- **Serial** (mono 400, 11px hub / 0.66–0.7rem sheet, `0.14–0.16em`, uppercase, `ink-faint`): anything that numbers or stamps rather than speaks.
- **Diagram lettering** (13.5 → 9px, set in SVG on the drawn plates): the only place type goes below 10px.

### Named Rules
**The Four Jobs Rule.** Each face has exactly one job: the grotesque at 700 engraves display, the grotesque at 400–650 runs the workbench, Faustina sets long-form reading, JetBrains Mono sets serials, figures and code. A face outside its job is a bug.

**The Serial Rule.** Anything that labels or numbers rather than speaks — plate facts, pills, captions, table heads, breadcrumbs, wayfinding, option keys, figcaptions — is mono, tracked `0.1–0.16em`, uppercase, in `ink-faint`. The eyebrow at the head of a lesson sheet is one of these: a right-aligned plate serial carrying the sheet's own identifier, not a label stacked above the title.

**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Figures line up everywhere, not only in tables: `print.css` applies `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to `table`, `.serial`, `.fig`, `time` and `.num` globally.

**The Two Roots Rule.** The reading sheet is set in rem against a 17px root; the workbench is set in px against a 15px body. Both ramps are real and neither is idealised — do not port one surface's steps onto the other, and do not "round" the half-pixel steps (13.5px, 14.5px, 11.5px) away: they are the shipped ramp.

**The Stand-In Rule.** Do not add an `@font-face` for Styrene A or Copernicus, and do not reference `anthropicSans` or `anthropicSerif`. They are licensed and absent. Any new face must be self-hosted under `course/assets/fonts/` with its licence file beside it.

## Layout

The hub is one centred column, `max-width: 68rem`, padded `24px 20px 96px`, with a masthead grid of `auto / minmax(0,1fr) / auto` (seal, title, counterfoil) that collapses to two columns below 760px. The reading sheet is a centred `62rem` page (`--page`) padded `4rem 2rem 8rem`. Both give way to the persistent side index: at widths above 64rem an open menu shifts the body by `--nav-w` (17rem, user-resizable); below that the menu overlays with a backdrop.

Spacing on the workbench is a 4px-based px rhythm (4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 18 / 20 / 22 / 24 / 34), with plates padded `20px 22px` and rows `16px 2px`. The reading sheet uses a rem rhythm instead (0.35 / 0.55 / 0.7 / 1.1 / 1.6 / 2.5 / 4rem). Breakpoints as shipped: 560px and 760px on the hub, 34rem and 64rem on the sheet, 720px for the rosette row, 64.01rem for the nav shift.

### Named Rules
**The One Edge Rule.** The lesson column is `--page` (50rem), owned solely by `lesson.css`. Every block on the sheet runs to the same column edge: `--prose` and `--measure` both resolve to 100%, so prose, figures, tables, callouts, apparatus, the pager and the live-reading blocks share one right-hand line. The two tokens remain as hooks and are still referenced by the selectors, but reintroducing a narrower value for either brings back the ragged right edge that was removed on 13 Sep 2026. Do not redefine them outside `lesson.css`.

**The Ledger Rule.** Three or more sibling items are ruled rows in one index sharing column tracks — `2px` top, `1px` between, `1px` bottom. If a design reaches for a grid of equal cards, it has left this world.

**The Printed Count Rule.** Progress is a printed count, not a bar: `.path-progress .track` is set to `display: none` and the number stands alone. Bounded measures that are genuinely quantities — the plan meter, the seven domain bars — are 1px-bordered tracks with a flat clay fill and a 1px olive target line at 72%. That is a ruled gauge, not a progress bar.

## Elevation & Depth

There is no elevation system. No `box-shadow`, no `text-shadow`, no `filter: drop-shadow`, no gradient appears anywhere in the build, in any state including hover and focus. Depth is done three ways: tonal steps of the plate (`plate` → `sheet` → `sheet-2`), rule weight (1px hairline versus 2px plate line), and, in the rosette, line density — more cuts read as more ink.

### Named Rules
**The No-Shadow Rule.** A surface that needs to read as raised gets `sheet` and a hairline. Nothing in this world casts.

**The Line-Density Rule.** Where tone is needed, cut more lines. Value is built from stroke count at 0.6px, never from fills or blurs.

## Shapes

Square, throughout. Every radius in the build is `0`, declared explicitly where a UA default would otherwise intrude (buttons, inputs, cards, pills, code, nav items, the focus outline), and `print.css` carries `figure.diagram rect, svg rect { rx: 0; ry: 0; }` so the drawn geometry obeys too. Borders are `1px` hairline or `2px` plate line; the reading sheet's base table rules also use a `1.5px` closing line under the last row.

The single exception is the radio input, which keeps `border-radius: 50%` on the control and its dot, so a single-choice control stays distinguishable from a checkbox at 15px. That exception is scoped to native radio controls and does not travel.

Curves exist, but only as drawn line work: the rosette's guilloche is a continuous interpolated curve, and the seal is a raster badge. Neither is a rounded box.

### Named Rules
**The Square Corner Rule.** Radius is `0` on every element, and `rx`/`ry` are `0` on every SVG rect. Radius is not a variable in this system.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** square (`0`), 1px `rule-firm` border.
- **Default:** `sheet` ground, `ink` text, grotesque 13px, padding `7px 13px`.
- **Primary:** the filled clay field — `ink-fill` ground and border, `ink-fill-t` text, weight 600, `0.01em` tracking.
- **Hover:** default borders and text go clay; primary *empties* — transparent ground, clay border and text. Disabled drops to 0.45 opacity with `rule`/`ink-faint`.
- **Serial button** (theme toggle): transparent, `rule` border, mono 11px uppercase tracked `0.14em`, padding `5px 9px`.
- **Focus:** the shared 2px `focus`-blue outline at 2px offset, from `print.css`. No component overrides it.

### Chips (pills)
- **Style:** transparent, 1px `rule-firm` border, mono 11px / 0.68rem uppercase tracked `0.14em`, `ink-faint`, padding `3px 7px`.
- **State:** `.on` switches border and text to clay. Never a filled chip.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** square (`0`).
- **Background:** `sheet`, or none at all — most groupings are ruled rows rather than plates.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** 1px `rule`; a plate that opens a section takes a 2px `rule-firm` top.
- **Internal Padding:** `20px 22px` on the hub, `1rem 1.2rem` on the sheet.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** transparent ground, square, and for the exam-date field a single 1px `rule-firm` underline rather than a box — mono 11.5px, with the datetime edit dimmed to `ink-faint` until focused or filled.
- **Checkbox / radio:** 15px, 1px `rule-firm`, transparent; a 7px clay dot scales in on check (`transform: scale(0 → 1)`, 0.12s ease-out) and the border goes clay. Radios are round; checkboxes are square.
- **Focus:** the shared blue outline.
- **Error:** an answered option takes a clay border when correct and an `oxblood` border when wrong, with its mono key letter recoloured to match.

### Navigation
- **Tabs:** a ledger index, not pills — a row bounded top and bottom by 1px `rule-firm`, each button transparent with a 2px transparent bottom border, grotesque 13.5px/500 in `ink-faint`. The selected tab goes `ink`, weight 650, with its bottom border in clay.
- **Side index:** fixed 17rem panel on `flag` ground with a 1px right rule, resizable by an 8px handle that shows a 2px clay line on hover or focus. Group heads are 0.66rem uppercase tracked labels; items are 0.84rem, `ink-soft`, with a mono number; hover takes the `code-bg` wash; the current page takes `accent-dim` with `ink` text and a clay number. A mono uppercase toggle sits top-left when the panel is closed; below 64rem an open panel darkens the page behind it.
- **Print:** the menu and toggle are removed entirely.

### Counterfoil (signature component)
A bordered block to the right of the masthead, min 246px: four equal cells divided by 1px hairlines, each a mono uppercase 11px `dt` over a 21px/600 tabular `dd`, carrying the exam's own facts (63 items, 120 minutes, 720 to pass, 7 domains). Under a 1px hairline sits the date bar, where the visitor's own exam date is typed into the underlined mono field and the days-to-go prints at 19px/600 beside it.

### Ledger Row
A four-track grid (`96px / 34px / minmax(0,1fr) / auto`) — mono serial, 30px line icon, title and prose, status pill — separated by 1px hairlines inside a 2px/1px bounded ledger. The next row up takes clay on its serial and icon only. Below 560px it folds to two tracks with the icon leading.

### Calibration Rosette (signature component)
A 600px canvas on a 1px `rule-firm` plate, drawn live from the published domain weights at up to 2× DPR, redrawn on `document.fonts.ready` and on theme change by reading the live custom properties off `:root`. It is cut in four passes: thirty full-turn guilloche lines in clay at 0.20 alpha (0.30 in light) as the always-present blank plate; then, per domain sector, a band of clay cuts from the floor radius out to the radius its accuracy reaches, with the distance from there to the pass ring banded in `gap`; then the 720 pass mark as a dashed olive ring (`[5,6]`, 1.2px) on the same radius scale; then each domain's mark and number in mono 15px/600 at the rim. Weighting is interpolated between neighbouring lobes because a rose engine cuts a continuous line and a stepped one would show as a seam. Line width is 0.6px throughout — tone is density.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take colour from the tokens in `print.css` only, and let the theme split handle light: `tint` for line work and text, `ink-fill` for a filled field.
- **Do** set every radius to `0`, and add `rx: 0; ry: 0` to any SVG rect you draw.
- **Do** use exactly two rule weights: 1px `rule` between peers, 2px `rule-firm` to open or close a structure.
- **Do** set anything that labels or numbers in mono, uppercase, tracked `0.14em`, in `ink-faint`.
- **Do** put three or more siblings in one ruled ledger sharing column tracks.
- **Do** let every block run to the column edge (`--prose` and `--measure` at 100%), and define both only in `lesson.css`.
- **Do** print a count where a progress bar is tempting.
- **Do** build tone from line density at 0.6px, and keep an empty state as unengraved plate.
- **Do** self-host any new face under `course/assets/fonts/` with its licence file, and keep the OpenDyslexic reading option working.
- **Do** leave the focus ring alone: 2px `focus` blue at 2px offset, from `print.css`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** introduce a second accent hue, or spend olive on anything but the 720 pass mark.
- **Don't** raise light-theme clay line work from `#ad5533` to `#d97757`; it measures 2.96:1 on ivory.
- **Don't** ship a `box-shadow`, `text-shadow`, `drop-shadow` or gradient in any state.
- **Don't** add a border radius, including on SVG rects and including "just for the avatar".
- **Don't** lay out equal-size cards in a grid where a ruled index belongs.
- **Don't** put more than one filled colour field on a sheet.
- **Don't** let ornament — rosette, guilloche, seal, plate borders — into a question body or a timed screen.
- **Don't** stack a lone section number above a heading; it goes inline, in mono, at `0.82em`.
- **Don't** declare an `@font-face` for Styrene A or Copernicus, or reference `anthropicSans` / `anthropicSerif`: they are licensed and not in this repo.
- **Don't** redefine `--prose`, `--measure` or any palette token outside the file that owns it, and don't delete the legacy aliases (`paper`, `accent`, `accent-dim`, `good`, `bad`, `flag`, `code-bg`, `grid`, `muted`, `text-secondary`, `success-text`, `critical`) — live markup and `nav.css` fallback chains still name them.
